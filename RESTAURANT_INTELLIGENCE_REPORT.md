# Restaurant intelligence report

## Executive view

DineIQ presents a single restaurant intelligence workflow from cleaned source records to menu, customer, demand and operations views. PKR values are formatted as currency, and the dashboard links each insight to a relevant evidence-backed route.

## Menu and customer intelligence

Menu profitability combines selling price and cost into contribution and margin measures, then classifies items for review. Customer RFM and basket tables support retention review and directional cross-sell candidates. The recommendation engine reports support, confidence and lift thresholds so users can distinguish evidence from a business-impact claim.

## Demand and operations

The forecast screen uses lag features for restaurant-hour demand and includes a historical baseline comparison. Inventory and wastage pages expose operational patterns; the wastage model is deliberately labelled as a screening signal because its test precision is low. Pricing and promotion pages describe observed historical associations and do not infer causal elasticity.

## User guidance

The authenticated Ask DineIQ assistant explains profit, margin, wastage, RFM, basket, forecast and data-quality concepts, and can look up loaded menu items and outlets. It is deterministic and grounded in reviewed formulas and loaded evidence rather than an unrestricted external model.

## Interpretation

The source data is synthetic, the canonical dashboard is historical v4, and the strict validation subset has not replaced the trained analytics artifacts. Review [ASSUMPTIONS_LIMITATIONS.md](ASSUMPTIONS_LIMITATIONS.md) for the complete scope boundary.
