# AI Usage Declaration

OpenAI Codex/ChatGPT assistance was used only for user-interface (UI) work and debugging support during development.

## What AI was used for

AI assistance was limited to:

- UI implementation, styling, and presentation improvements
- Debugging assistance, issue investigation, and troubleshooting suggestions

## What AI was not used for

AI was not used for data generation, analytical or machine-learning results, benchmark results, production deployment claims, or fabrication of project evidence.

The project's core implementation, data, analysis, model outputs, and verification results remain the responsibility of the project team and were independently reviewed where applicable.
